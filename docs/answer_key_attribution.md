## 2. How the truth is built
- Each person draws one uniform U. [0, p0) is the baseline; each touch owns the next stretch of length `lift`, in time order.
  The stretch U falls into is the true cause. A rerun deletes one channel's stretches without moving the others.
- Baseline propensity p0 = expit(a + 1.3 z), z ~ N(0,1); `a` calibrated so expected baseline revenue = CSV year-1 Direct_Other revenue.
  p0 mean 0.050, median 0.026, 99th pct 0.351.
- Touch lift = lambda_c x (1 - p0) x 0.7^(j-1), j = j-th touch of that channel for the person. Direct touches have zero lift.
  lambda: Google 0.0287, Meta 0.0093, Affiliates 0.0288, TikTok 0.0031, SEO 0.0140, Email 0.0148.
- **Lift cap:** if p0 + sum(lifts) > 1, all of that person's lifts are scaled by (1 - p0) / sum(lifts), so the total is exactly 1.
  People capped: 0 of 250,000. Largest uncapped total: 0.942; largest total after the cap: 0.941558.
- Selection bias: extra touches ~ Poisson(mu x exp(gamma z - gamma^2/2)), so high-gamma types reach high-propensity people.

- Every person has one entry touch at day 0 (mix: Google generic 30%, Meta prospecting 20%, TikTok prospecting 10%, SEO organic 20%, Direct direct 15%, Affiliates affiliate 5%).
- Zero-lift navigational session just before 70% of orders (Direct direct 45%, Google brand 30%, SEO organic 15%, Email newsletter 10%). Paid ones (Google brand, Email, SEO) carry cost.
- Cost per touch = year-1 spend of that market x channel / that cell's touches (including paid navigational clicks).

## 3. Paths and lags
- Self-serve: causing touch -> order, log-normal median 31 days, sigma 0.65, truncated at 91 days (as in `generate_data.py`).
- Lead path (24.3% of people: all summer houses + 11% of outdoor furniture): touch -> quote_request log-normal median 7d, sigma 0.8, cap 60d;
  quote_request -> order median 60d, sigma 1.0, **truncated at 365 days** (the repo's 91-day cap does not apply here).
- Lead people with no cause request a quote with probability 0.372, so about 27% of quotes end in an order.
- Orders censored by the data end (2026-10-25): 2 in the factual run (the same cut-off applies in every rerun).

## 4. Consent loss (observed paths only)
Per person: DE/AT 30%, FR/NL 20%, UK 15%, SE 10% untracked. An untracked person's touches are all missing from observed
paths (`tracked = 0`); purchases and quote requests are always recorded (server-side / CRM). The truth uses full paths.
